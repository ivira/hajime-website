What's different

Most dictionaries confuse translation with transliteration. In Hajime “Seiken-tsuki” is not just “seiken-tsuki” spelled out — it's a straight punch with the fist. The quizzes test real understanding, not the ability to repeat syllables.

No account, no ads, no analytics, no data collection. Every term already lives inside the app. Free forever.
