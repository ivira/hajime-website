Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 May 2026

一

In short

Hajime does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. Everything that happens in the app stays on your device.

二

What exactly we don't collect

  • We don't collect your name, email, phone number or any contact details.
  • We don't use analytics, event tracking or advertising identifiers.
  • We don't send anything to servers — the app works fully offline.
  • We don't use cookies (there simply aren't any, because there's no web component).
  • We don't require you to create an account.
三

App data

All dictionary terms ship with the app as local files. Quiz progress exists only within the current session — nothing is saved once you leave a quiz.

The app requests no system permissions whatsoever (camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, notifications).

四

Third-party services

Hajime uses no third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, crash reporting or any other purpose. The only “external” channel is iOS's built-in app-update mechanism through the App Store, operated by Apple under its own policy.

五

Children

The app is not aimed exclusively at children, but it's completely safe for any age: it collects no data and contains no ads or external links to third parties.

六

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated text will be published on this page with a new “last updated” date. We won't send any in-app notifications, since there's no channel for them.

七

Contact

Questions about privacy or the app in general — write to vira@tkachenko.kiev.ua.
